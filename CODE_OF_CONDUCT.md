# A welcoming Roost

Batbox welcomes people of every background and experience level. Be kind, be
clear, and leave room for someone else to learn.

- Discuss the work with curiosity and respect. Critique ideas and behavior, not identities.
- Ask before assuming. Explain technical feedback without belittling people.
- Respect boundaries and privacy. Do not publish someone else’s private information.
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual attention without consent, and
  repeated personal attacks are not welcome.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces maintained by the project. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content,
limit participation, or remove participants when needed. Responses should be
proportionate to the behavior and its effect on others.

To report a concern, contact a listed maintainer through a private contact method
on their GitHub profile. Include relevant links and what happened. If the concern
involves that maintainer, contact another maintainer where one is available.
Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

We build better when people feel safe enough to ask questions and try things.
